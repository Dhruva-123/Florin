Florin - A market simulator for beginners to practice trading

This platform is being built for future traders, today's investors to understand the market better and gain knowledge without losing a penny. 
(Note : None of the trades you will be doing with us will reflect in real wealth nor will it obtain you real stock in said company or organization. All the trading will happen with a currency called Florin that exists only in our simulator. This is meant for meant for practice but doesn't reflect the market 100%. We are not liable for any loses.)

Features
- Users can trade in a free market which is run by both AI agents and also other real users. Market contains 2000+ stocks, including but not limited to, `*Amazon`, `*Microsoft`, `*Nvidia` etc. 
- Gain real-time insights via Financial news and trade with `Florin` given to you!
- Add limits to when and at how much you want to sell or buy your stock.
- Invest into *Baskets(SIP) monthly with automated payments and watch your stock grow!
- Practice aggressive day-trading statergies without fear with our `quick-news` feature that correlates world events to a bump or slump in the value of a particular stock.
